# ILSA Canonical Codebook (Appendix)

## Academic_Resilience

**Examples:** Academic resilience (binary: reading Level 3+ vs below) using 10 reading PVs | Academic resilience status (ARS=1 vs NRS=0) | Academic resilience classification (ARS vs NRS; binary)

**Definition:** Binary or latent resilience indicators (resilient vs non-resilient) in ILSA non-cognitive research.

## Anxiety_Stress

**Examples:** Work stress (composite factor score) | Workplace stress (sub-dimension) | Student behavioral stress (sub-dimension)

**Definition:** Test anxiety, stress, fear of failure

## Assessment_Methodology

**Examples:** Automated scoring agreement (accuracy) vs human scoring | Automated scoring agreement (Cohen’s kappa) vs human scoring | Hallucination rate in automated scoring

**Definition:** Test design, scoring validation, DIF/Q-matrix, and automated vs human scoring agreement.

## Assessment_Practices

**Examples:** Accountability index | Accountability

**Definition:** Testing practices, assessment policy (non-PV outcome)

## Belonging_School_Climate

**Examples:** student behavior hindering learning achievement | Sense of school belonging | Academic achievement via mediators (attitude and belonging)

**Definition:** Belonging, bullying, disciplinary climate, safety

## Civic_Achievement

**Examples:** Civic engagement profile membership (disengaged/moderately engaged/engaged) | Trust in civic institutions | Civic learning at school

**Definition:** Civic knowledge, citizenship, ICCS outcomes

## Civic_Engagement

**Examples:** Private-sphere environmental actions (classification) | Public-sphere environmental actions (classification) | Awareness of intercultural communication

**Definition:** Political/civic participation, voting, multicultural attitudes

## Curriculum_Instruction

**Examples:** Language skills in native curriculum (predicted from math language themes) | School lessons per week | Same-ability groups

**Definition:** Instructional time, curriculum, tracking, streaming

## Dropout_Retention

**Examples:** School assessments for retention/promotion | Truancy | Assessments for retention/promotion

**Definition:** Dropout risk, retention, early school leaving

## Gender_Demographics

**Examples:** predicts mathematics achievement measured by pv1math–pv10math from gender | DIF direction by gender (favoring males vs females) | Academic resilience positive prediction rate disparity by gender

**Definition:** Student sex/gender, age, and grade cohort indicators.

## Homework_Study_Time

**Examples:** Academic achievement heterogeneity by learning time (quantiles) | Mathematics learning time | Studies/does homework before class

**Definition:** Homework, study time, learning time

## ICT_Usage

**Examples:** Digital skills (CIL) via ICT affect mediation | DIF presence across ICT items (uniform DIF via GPCMlasso) | Digital Sports participation (binary)

**Definition:** Student ICT access, frequency of use, and digital learning engagement (ICTRES, ENCTUSE).

## Immigration_Language

**Examples:** immigration status achievement | Language at home | Other language at home

**Definition:** Immigrant status, language at home, migration background

## Language_Proficiency

**Examples:** Language and communication level (I–IV) | Reading fluency

**Definition:** Language proficiency, reading fluency as predictor

## Math_Achievement

**Examples:** Mathematical achievement class (PV1MATH) | Low vs High mathematics proficiency (binary) | Math achievement (country-level mean; residual screening)

**Definition:** Mathematics proficiency on ILSA scales (e.g. PISA PV1MATH–PV10MATH, TIMSS numeracy).

## Metacognition_Strategies

**Examples:** Metacognition (understanding, remembering) | Metacognitive summarizing awareness | Metacognition: credibility assessment

**Definition:** Learning strategies, metacognition, self-regulation

## Occupational_Expectation

**Examples:** Expected occupational position

**Definition:** Career expectations, occupational plans

## Parental_Involvement

**Examples:** Parents’ emotional support | Mother's education level | Father’s education

**Definition:** Parent support, involvement, homework help

## Peer_Effects

**Examples:** Teacher nonverbal immediacy (NVI) — variance decomposition across teacher, lesson, and situation | Harder than classmates | Enjoy classmates’ success

**Definition:** Peer composition, classroom aggregates

## Prior_Achievement

**Examples:** Link new knowledge with prior knowledge | Prior achievement (final mark) | Class % low prior achievement

**Definition:** Prior scores, earlier grades, lagged achievement

## Problem_Solving_Achievement

**Examples:** Creative thinking performance (average of 10 plausible values) | Creative thinking performance (PVs; standardized regression effects) | Creative thinking score (PVs pooled via Rubin’s rules)

**Definition:** Problem solving, creative thinking assessments

## Process_Data_Log

**Examples:** Latent speed (log response time) | Differential response time (DRT) between ML and non-ML | Total time on task

**Definition:** Log-file, response time, process indicators

## Psychometrics_Process

**Examples:** Student math proficiency (EAP ability estimates) | Item3 response (Awacom) | SE71054 correctness (human vs ANN)

**Definition:** Psychometric scaling, latent traits (WLE/theta/EAP), item-response and process-data indicators (LPA profiles, PCCR/ACCR, measurement invariance, latent speed, log actions).

## Reading_Achievement

**Examples:** High vs low reading literacy (binary) | Reading Literacy | Reading proficiency (Low vs Others)

**Definition:** Reading literacy proficiency (PISA/PIRLS reading scales and text comprehension outcomes).

## SES

**Examples:** Dominant response processes (cliques of action patterns) | ESCS achievement | Solution-strategy class membership (5 latent classes)

**Definition:** OECD/IEA composite socioeconomic background (ESCS, HOMEPOS, HISEI, PARED) capturing family wealth, parental education, and occupational status.

## School_Efficiency_Climate

**Examples:** School inefficiency score (DDF/DEA output) | School effectiveness (high vs low; dichotomous residual-based target) | Relationship between within-school average achievement and within-school inequality (within-school SD vs within-school mean)

**Definition:** School efficiency, inequality, leadership, collaboration climate

## School_Resources

**Examples:** Class size (average) | Class size | Student-teacher ratio

**Definition:** School material resources, shortages, facilities

## School_Type_Composition

**Examples:** School location (rural=1) | School type (public/private) | School type

**Definition:** Public/private, urban/rural, school type

## Science_Achievement

**Examples:** Poor vs better science proficiency (Level ≤1b vs ≥1a) | Science achievement quartile (Q1–Q4) | science achievement

**Definition:** Science literacy/achievement (PISA/TIMSS science PVs and domain scores).

## Self_Efficacy

**Examples:** General self-efficacy (continuous IRT score) | Science self-efficacy (SCIEEFF; WLE) | Science self-efficacy (WLE index)

**Definition:** Student self-efficacy, self-concept, confidence

## Student_Motivation

**Examples:** Students’ intrinsic motivation (STU_MOTI) | Non-cognitive skills composite score (NCS) | Enjoyment of school (latent/scale score)

**Definition:** Student interest, intrinsic motivation, value beliefs, and self-concept toward learning domains in ILSA student questionnaires.

## System_Policy

**Examples:** Country GDP per capita | Social-emotional skills policy | GDP (total economic size)

**Definition:** Country/system policy, GDP, tracking age, expenditure

## Teacher_Efficacy_Workforce

**Examples:** Workload stress (sub-dimension) | Teacher job satisfaction (TALIS scale) | Socioemotional support (encouragement and warmth; observer-rated) predicted by NVI

**Definition:** TALIS/PISA teacher workforce constructs: instructional and classroom-management efficacy (ECM/ESE), job satisfaction, intention to quit, cognitive activation (COGAC), PLC, and organisational innovativeness — process drivers distinct from student achievement.

## Teacher_Quality_Practices

**Examples:** Instructional use of AI (past 12 months; yes/no) | Instructional use of AI (past 12 months; yes/no) — moderation by country group | Specific AI instructional application modes (8 binary outcomes; yes/no)

**Definition:** Teaching practices, instructional support, classroom climate, and teacher qualifications.

## Theoretical_and_Meta_Synthesis

**Examples:** Literature synthesis outcome (not student-level prediction) | Derived binary variables (including item outcomes) | 288 schools) achievement

**Definition:** Non-empirical synthesis rows: systematic reviews, methodology/policy discourse, heuristic migration placeholders, and literature-level outcomes without student-level effect-size targets — isolated from empirical achievement/motivation boxes for RAG.

## Uncategorized_Contextual

**Examples:** Item7 response (Awacom) | Automated CNN score classification vs human rater (dichotomous/partial credit as applicable) | Harmonized test scores (Tscore_ML)

**Definition:** Context-specific label that could not be aligned after primary + smart-domain rules; requires manual mapping for RAG retrieval.

## Wellbeing_Mental_Health

**Examples:** Life satisfaction (0–10 scale) | Positive affect (SWBP) | Negative affect (SWBN)

**Definition:** Student well-being, anxiety, stress, and life satisfaction constructs in ILSA surveys.

## Wellbeing_Psychological

**Examples:** Mediation via teaching enjoyment | Meaning in life | Meaning in life (eudaemonia)

**Definition:** Psychological traits and socio-emotional outcomes (non-clinical)
